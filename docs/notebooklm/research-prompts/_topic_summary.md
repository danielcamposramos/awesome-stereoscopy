prompt, including its initial brief, project provenance, evidence rules and
deliverables; no shared preamble is required. All six are under 3,000 characters.
Copy the entire file into the research input. Soft wrapping in an editor does
not add line breaks.

replacements. These are research instructions, not research findings. The
[combined earlier brief](old_attempts/combined-research-brief.md) is retained
for history; use the individual prompts for new runs.
