# Stereo video on YouTube: where it is and how to find it

YouTube still hosts a large body of stereoscopic video. [Its help page still documents uploading 3D](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7278886), but the player's own 3D viewing modes are gone (see [Services that were switched off](readme.md#services-that-were-switched-off)). The videos stayed, so this page points to where they are. It links only to public playlists and videos on YouTube itself, and copies nothing.

## How to watch them today

- **Side-by-side (SBS) on a 3D TV:** play full screen, then pick side-by-side in the TV's own 3D menu. The browser cannot switch the TV into 3D, so this step is always manual.
- **SBS in a headset or phone viewer:** Cardboard-style viewers, Gear VR and display glasses such as the XREAL Air show each half to one eye. Full-width SBS (3840×1080) is made for display glasses with an SBS mode.
- **Anaglyph:** red/cyan glasses on any screen. Check the colour pair in the title: some videos are red/green or amber/blue, and the wrong glasses mix the eyes.

## How to find more: the yt3d tags

YouTube's 3D player was [a Google employee's 20% project, launched in July 2009](http://googlesystem.blogspot.com/2009/07/youtube-3d.html). Uploaders switched it on with tags in the video's metadata:

- `yt3d:enable=true` - turned on the 3D viewer.
- `yt3d:aspect=16:9` (or `4:3`, `8:9`, …) - the aspect ratio of the encoded video.
- `yt3d:swap=true` - the left and right images are swapped, the usual case for cross-eyed layouts.
- `yt3d:metadata=user` - seen on many later uploads; not documented in any source found for this page.

The tags still sit in the videos' metadata. Daniel, who maintains this list, found most of the playlists below with one trick: **search for `yt3d` together with a second term.** Add the second term with care:

- `yt3d 3D` finds a lot, but "3D" also matches computer-generated animation, which is not stereo.
- More precise terms: `SBS`, `side by side`, `anaglyph`, `red cyan`, `stereoscopic`, `full SBS`, `3840x1080`.
- A stereo camera's name finds real stereo footage: `QooCam EGO` is how the full-width playlist below was found.
- Then open the playlists that the results belong to. People who collect stereo video tend to collect a lot of it.

## Playlists

Public playlists curated by viewers. Checked 25 September 2026; playlists change, so the counts will drift.

- [Red blue glasses 3D videos](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLjIhRoUOmiLABls4iP1-LHyRPvqZ2Vt9o) - 25 anaglyph videos, mostly red/cyan with some red/green: animation, a Solar System tour from mission imagery, an M. C. Escher-inspired piece, physics simulations. Opens with an animation that has close to 5 million views.
- [Videos em 3D - Saindo da tela](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLQ9jKfdd0y6u-tD5V4Du_k10e76D-yI3F) - 34 anaglyph videos collected in Brazil ("coming out of the screen"), from a 2009 upload with over 3 million views to a roller-coaster film made for paper glasses.
- [3D SBS videos - best effects compilation](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLXN-kZlFgjEh45lUIKNxLz9OEqkPeQ0jv) - 18 side-by-side videos: roller coasters, underwater footage, a parachute jump, and a series of city and national-park tours, one of them with binaural audio. Opens with a 2013 demo that has passed 17 million views.
- [3840x1080 SBS 3D Xreal Air](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLoaRp1LIj9jJdL-Q44bELZz_628uxxnql) - 25 full-width side-by-side videos for display glasses, most of them shot on the Kandao QooCam EGO stereo camera: city walks, aquariums, plants and animals. Several uploaders offer the original files for download.
- [PC VR Games and Mods - VR Box SBS 3D Videos](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLoQ9rLKiGFRIsntVC5dBMLmQi8S6gkb1Q) - 66 side-by-side captures of PC games played in VR, many through mods such as UEVR: Half-Life 2, GTA V and San Andreas, Red Dead Redemption 2, Flight Simulator 2024, Portal 2. Useful for seeing what stereo does in games before trying it.

Three other large playlists found the same way are deliberately not linked, because they mix in copies of studio 3D films uploaded by people who do not own them. The studios' films belong on Blu-ray 3D, not on this page.

## History: a creator's 3D on YouTube, 2010-2011

The earliest stereo uploads found for this page are hobby and test clips from 2009, the year the player launched. The earliest work by a major creator found is Joe Penna's (MysteryGuitarMan), the Brazilian YouTuber who later directed [Arctic](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arctic_(film)) and [Stowaway](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stowaway_(2021_film)). Daniel, who maintains this list, had pitched 3D and the yt3d tags to him through YouTube's private messages, which no longer exist ([how this list began](https://github.com/danielcamposramos/sony-bravia-linux/blob/main/docs/3d-origin-story.md)). In order:

- [3D Test](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KE-KUPJzxwU) - 30 December 2010, on his second channel (then youtube.com/jp, now renamed). 37 seconds, tagged `yt3d:enable=true`.
- [Maestro Psychedelico](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4nuSwheTsgg) - 20 January 2011. The first edit, in 2D.
- [How to watch 3D videos on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pNuKAaC337U) - 1 February 2011. His guide to the old player's 3D menu, published twelve minutes before the video it was made for.
- [3D Maestro](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uMTvaNomlWY) - 1 February 2011, main channel, over 2 million views. Tagged `yt3d:enable=true` and `yt3d:swap=true`.
- [3D with Glasses](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ll9hIWRp7gc) - 2 June 2011, on the second channel.
- [MysteryGuitarMan: Bring Music to Life with 3D Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VhNgOhaHNtY) - 11 November 2011, on Howdini's channel. By then he was the 3D expert in a sponsored how-to series.

Watch the guide with the player in mind: every mode it walks through is one the ordinary YouTube player no longer offers.
